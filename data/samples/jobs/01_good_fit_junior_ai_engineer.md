# Junior AI Engineer (LLM Applications)

**Company:** Lumen Health GmbH
**Location:** Berlin, Germany (hybrid, 2 days per week in the office)
**Salary:** €52,000 – €60,000 per year
**Language:** English (our working language). German is a plus, not required.
**Visa:** We can support visa applications for non-EU candidates.

## About us
Lumen Health builds software that helps clinics answer patient questions faster. Our 40-person
team is shipping an AI assistant that reads clinic guidelines and drafts answers for nurses.

## What you will do
- Build and improve LLM features with Python, LangChain and the OpenAI API.
- Design retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines over medical guidelines.
- Work with vector databases (we use ChromaDB and pgvector).
- Write evaluation sets and measure answer quality and faithfulness.
- Build internal tools and demos with Streamlit or FastAPI.

## What we are looking for
**Must have**
- 1+ years of hands-on Python
- Experience building at least one LLM or RAG application (side projects count)
- Understanding of embeddings, chunking and vector search
- Clear written communication in English

**Nice to have**
- Docker and CI/CD (GitHub Actions)
- Experience with LLM evaluation tools (e.g. DeepEval, Ragas)
- A background outside tech: we value career changers
- Some German

## What we offer
Mentoring from senior engineers, a €1,500 learning budget, 30 vacation days.
