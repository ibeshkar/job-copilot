# Senior Machine Learning Engineer – Computer Vision (w/m/d)

**Unternehmen / Company:** Rheinwerk Automotive AG
**Location:** Munich, Germany (on-site, 5 days per week)
**Salary:** €95,000 – €115,000
**Visa:** Not offered.

## About the role
You will lead the development of perception models for our driver-assistance systems and mentor
a team of 4 ML engineers.

## Your tasks
- Design and train deep learning models for object detection and segmentation.
- Optimise models for embedded GPUs (CUDA, TensorRT).
- Own the MLOps platform on Kubernetes (Kubeflow, MLflow).
- Present results to customers and works councils in German.

## Your profile
**Must have**
- 6+ years of experience in machine learning, at least 3 in computer vision
- Expert in PyTorch and modern CNN/transformer architectures
- Experience with CUDA and model optimisation for edge devices
- Kubernetes and MLOps in production
- **German at C1 level or higher** (daily communication with customers and works council)
- Master's degree or PhD in computer science, electrical engineering or similar

**Nice to have**
- Experience in automotive (ISO 26262, ASPICE)
- Publications at CVPR, ICCV or similar
