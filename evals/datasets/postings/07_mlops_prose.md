# MLOps Engineer at Fieldsense

Fieldsense helps farmers predict crop disease from satellite images. We are 18 people in
Copenhagen and fully remote across the EU.

We're looking for an MLOps engineer to take our models from notebooks to reliable services.
You'll need solid Python, real experience deploying machine learning models to production, and
day-to-day comfort with Docker and Kubernetes. You should also have worked with at least one
cloud platform, ideally GCP, though AWS is fine too. It would be great if you've used Terraform
or know your way around model monitoring tools such as Evidently, but we're happy to teach that.
Our working language is English.

Salary: DKK 600,000 – 720,000. We sponsor visas.
